# Decision records

One file per decision, kept here, for every decision prepared for the owner to rule on in this project.

## The shape

A record is one page at most and carries five sections, in this order:

1. **Name and status.** The decision named in one line, in plain words. Status is Proposed until the owner rules, then Ruled, with the date of the ruling.
2. **Context.** How things stand today, in plain words, with the real current values, each one checked against the page, the code or the record it came from.
3. **Options.** Each real option with one concrete case, its good sides and its bad sides stated plainly. The status quo stands as an option when keeping things as they are is a real choice. Anything unresolved is written as an explicit NEEDS CLARIFICATION marker, and a record carrying one does not move forward.
4. **Scope.** Whether the same question lives anywhere else, what was checked and what the answer was.
5. **Recommendation.** Labelled as the recommendation and argued from the cases in the Options section. It comes last in the record.

A decision with a running cost carries the money, measured, in its Context and in each option: the rate and its source, the figure at today's sizes, any free allowance that applies, and what the figure becomes as the thing grows.

## The gate

Before a record is presented, a checker who did not write it reads it as a hostile reviewer and adds a check section at the end of the file: the sources opened, by name; what the checker corrected; and the answers to three tests. Could a person who has never seen the project's files restate the problem from the Context alone? Is there a specific reason each other option loses? Is at least one cost visible? A record without a completed check section is not presented.

## At ruling time

The record gains the outcome, written as "chosen, because\u2026", and a Consequences section. The record, not chat, is where the ruling lives afterwards, and the ruling is entered in DECISIONS.md at the root, in the owner's own words, on the day it is given.

## Names

Files here are named for their decision: decision-<short-name>.md.

The canonical text of the Decision Records Policy stands in the agents-policy repo at docs/decision-records-policy.md. This page states the keeping rule for this repo; where the two disagree, the policy and the owner's most recent ruling govern.
