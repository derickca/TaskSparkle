Terms

The agreement between you and TaskSparkle, in plain language. Privacy stands inside it.

Draft: this page is a working draft and is not published yet.

The short version

  • Your boards are yours. They are private, and only you and the agents you give a key can reach them.
  • The service is free. There is no card and no trial.
  • There are no passwords. You sign in with a link we email you, and with a passkey if you add one.

Who can join

Joining is open to people in Canada and the United States. Using TaskSparkle means agreeing to these terms.

Your account

  • You sign in with a link we email you. The link goes to the email address on your account, and only someone who can read that email can use it.
  • Add a passkey and that device signs in with one tap.
  • Keep your email account and your passkeys safe. What happens under your account is your responsibility until you tell us the account is compromised.
  • You can close your account at any time, in Settings under Account. Closing it deletes your boards and everything under them.

Your boards

  • Your boards belong to you. We hold them so the service can store them and show them back to you and your agents. That is the whole of it.
  • Nobody else can see your boards. A board has no public view.

Agent keys

  • A key acts as you on your boards. It can read and change everything you can.
  • You answer for what your keys do.
  • A key shows once, when you make it. Keep it in your agent's secret store and nowhere else.
  • You can revoke a key at any time in Settings. Revoking stops that key at once, and your other keys keep working.

Privacy

What we hold. Your name and your email address. Your boards and everything on them. The passkeys you add. A record of the sign-in links we email you. Feedback you send us.

Who else touches it. Cloudflare hosts the site and stores its data. Our mail service sends your sign-in links.

How long it is kept. Your information is kept while your account stands. When you close your account it is deleted, and copies inside backups age out within 14 days. Feedback is kept up to six months.

Your choices. See it: your account pages show your information. Delete it: close your account. Ask: write to the privacy officer.

Privacy officer: Derick Campbell, certifiablemedia@outlook.com.

The rules

  • Do not use TaskSparkle to break the law.
  • Do not try to reach another person's boards, keys or account.
  • Do not attack the service or overload it.
  • We can close an account that breaks these rules.
  • A ban ends your sign-in. A ban comes with no reason given and no appeal.

Liability

TaskSparkle is never liable for indirect losses. If you have a dispute with TaskSparkle, start by writing to us through the Feedback page and sharing what went wrong. If that does not settle it, the courts of British Columbia decide, under the laws of British Columbia and Canada.

Changes

The current terms are the ones posted on this page, and a change applies once it is posted here. If you cannot accept a change, close your account.
