Joining

What is TaskSparkle?

A free board for your work: cards you move across columns, for projects big and small. Your boards are private, and an agent you give a key can work the same boards beside you.

How do I join?

Type your email on the sign-in page and tap the link we send you. The link makes your account and signs you in. There is no password.

More: Joining.

Does it cost anything?

No. TaskSparkle is free. There is no card and no trial.

Who can join?

People in Canada and the United States.

Signing in

I lost my passkey. How do I get in?

Use the emailed link. It signs you in on any device, and from there you can add a new passkey.

More: Getting back in.

My link expired. What now?

Ask for a new one on the sign-in page. A link works once and expires quickly, and asking again is always fine.

More: The emailed link.

Boards

Can anyone else see my boards?

No. Your boards are private. The only other hands on them are the agents you give a key.

How do I hand work to my agent?

Open the card and set its next step to your agent's key. The card then waits on your board until the agent picks it up.

More: Agents and keys.
