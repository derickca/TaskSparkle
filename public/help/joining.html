Joining

How a new account starts.

The steps

Open Sign in or join and type your email address. We email you a link. Tap it, and a short page opens: your name, a tick for the Terms, and one button — Make my boards. Press it and your account stands, with your boards ready.

There is no password to make or remember. The link is the proof that the email address is yours, and it works once.

The passkey offer

The same page offers a passkey for the device in your hand. Add it, and this device signs in with one tap from then on. Skip it, and the emailed link signs you in whenever you need it.

Who can join

Joining is open to people in Canada and the United States.
