Getting back in

Being shut out is a short problem, not a lost account.

The way back

On the sign-in page, ask for a link. It goes to the email address on your account. Tap it and you are signed in — whatever happened to your passkey, your device or your browser. Once you are in, add a fresh passkey for the device in your hand.

Why email is the proof

The link can only be read by someone who can open that inbox, so the service treats tapping it as proof the account is yours. That is why your account stands or falls with that address: if you can no longer read the inbox on your account, there is no other way in. It is the same rule that keeps everyone else out of your boards.
