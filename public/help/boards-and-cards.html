Boards and cards

The pieces, and how a card moves.

The pieces

  • A board is one project or one part of your life. You can hold many boards, and each is private.
  • A board is a row of columns — a new board starts with Inbox, Ready, Doing, Waiting and Done. Rename, add, remove and reorder them in Settings.
  • A card is one piece of work. It holds a title, notes, a due date, a checklist of steps, links and comments.
  • Labels belong to one board and colour a card. A project groups cards across a board, and the filter row narrows the board to one project.

Working a card

Select the plus in a column to make a card there. Drag a card to move it on a desktop; on a phone, drag from the six-dot grip in its corner, or use the Move buttons in its editor. There is no Save button: every edit saves itself as you make it. Archive a card to put it away without deleting it; archiving keeps its steps, links and comments.

Handing work over

Every card names whose turn its next step is: yours, one of your agent keys, or no one. Open the card and use the Next step chooser to hand it over; a comment you write at the same time posts with the hand-over. A card waiting on an agent wears a chip with the agent's name.

Finding a card

Search in the header looks through card titles, notes and checklist steps across all your boards. A card can also move to another board: the editor's Move board button asks for its board, column and project and names any labels that would be left behind before you confirm.
