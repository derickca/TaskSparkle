One family, six products

The Spark products share one spark logo, one layout and one dark blue on off-white light style. Each gets its own accent colour. GrabASpot keeps its own logo. The seven-colour bar is for splashes of colour in any of them.

Accent colours

Each product's own colour

Each product is shown in its light style and its dark style. The accent fills the logo tile, the main button, chips and a thin top line. Headings and text stay dark blue in light and near-white in dark in every product, so the family still reads as one. Second buttons are outlined. Accent colours sit behind text, never as text on the page.

Splashes of colour

The seven-colour bar

Any product can use these for charts, illustrations, celebrations, dividers and empty states. Whether they also colour extra buttons or tags is each product's own choice. The figures show which text colour can sit on each one; the check is worked out live from the colours on this page.
