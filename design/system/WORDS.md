# TaskSparkle words

The words TaskSparkle uses for its own things, gathered from the shipped pages, the guides, the app and the agent directions as they stand. Check new wording against this list before a page ships. Where a pair is settled, write the first and never the second.

The prose checker in tests/local-checks.mjs is this list's mechanical half: it keeps machinery words out of reader text, and its pins hold several of the phrases below word for word.

## The pieces

- **board** — one project or one part of a person's life; a row of columns. A person holds many boards, and each is private.
- **card** — one piece of work on a board. A card holds a title, notes, a checklist, links, comments, files and a due date.
- **column** — one stage on a board. A new board starts with five: Inbox, Ready, Doing, Waiting and Done.
- **label** — a colour on a card. Labels belong to one board, and a card can wear more than one.
- **project** — a group of cards on one board. The filter row narrows a board to one project, and a card carries one project or none.

Boards, labels and projects are three distinct dimensions, and their names never trade places: a label is not a tag and a project is not a category.

A card's parts: **checklist** (its entries are **steps**), **links**, **comments**, **files**, **due date**, and the **next step** — whose turn the card's next step is: the person's, one of their agent keys, or no one's.

## Names

- **TaskSparkle** — the product. The hosted copy is the **live service** and the code anyone can run is the **open source repo**; both phrases stand in full.
- **MemberSpark ID** — the sign-in. Its button says **Sign in with MemberSpark**.
- **Your own AI** — the Settings page and the Learn guide where a person gives an agent a key. Its standing words: "Make a key so your own AI assistant can read or work in a Spark product for you, and revoke it any time."
- **agent key** — the key itself, made at MemberSpark under Your account, then AI keys. Work an agent makes wears its key's name, and a key's waiting cards stand in its **queue**.
- **Help** — the door in the header. **Learn** — the guides shelf inside Help. The guides: Joining, The emailed link, Passkeys, Getting back in, Boards and cards, Your own AI.
- **Feedback** — the page and the door in the header and footer. Its kinds: Something I love, Something to improve, Something else.
- **Settings** — its pages stand in this order: Theme, Boards, Columns, Labels, Projects, Your own AI, then Admin for administrators alone. **Account** stands alone, outside Settings.
- **TaskSparkle Ops** — the operator surface. Its sections: Overview, Joining, Statistics, New signups, People, Sign-in record.
- **looks** — the eight themes: Standard, Comic, Console, Pretty, Artistic, Sunset, Forest and Midnight. The theme modes are Default, Light and Dark.
- **Pilot** — the service stands in a pilot, and the header says so.

## Acts

- **Sign in or join** — the one button on the door. The joining page's own button is **Make my account**.
- **sign out**, and **sign out everywhere** for every device at once.
- **hand over** — set a card's next step to an agent key. A comment written at the same time posts with the **hand-over**, and the holder **picks up** the card with its first write on it.
- **archive** — put a card away without deleting it. Archiving is not deleting: an archived card keeps its steps, links, labels and files. **Restore** brings it back.
- **follow** a card, **copy** a board, **save as a template** and **start** from one. A column's **recurring list** refills itself on its schedule.
- There is no Save button: every edit saves itself as it is made.

## Standing phrases

- The front page: "A free board for your work — and your AI works it beside you."
- "Free. No card, no trial clock." There is no card and no trial.
- "Your boards are private."
- The **emailed link** signs a person in: it works once and expires in 15 minutes, and asking again is always fine. A **passkey** signs a device in with one tap.
- The footer: "By MemberSpark, a BC non-profit" with the flag, then the motto "Crafted by makers, for makers."
- "Rather run it yourself? TaskSparkle is open source."
- While the pilot stands, the titles read **Privacy (draft)** and **Terms of Use (draft)**, each page carries "Draft: This page is a working draft for the pilot.", and the agreement words carry "The terms are a draft while the pilot stands."
- A test account's banner: "Viewing as a test account. Read-only."
- A local key tried on the live service is refused with: "This key's road has moved: agent keys live at MemberSpark now. Sign in with MemberSpark and make a key there, then try again."
- The agent directions open "TaskSparkle directions for agents" and call the product "a personal kanban board" — the one place the word kanban stands.

## Settled pairs

Write the first, never the second:

- **sign in** — never log in
- **Sign in or join** — never Sign up or Register
- **the emailed link** — never magic link
- **card** — never ticket
- **label** — never tag; **project** — never category
- **archive** a card to put it away — never delete for that act
- **hand over** a card — never assign it
- **live service** and **open source repo**, in full — never shortened
- **Your own AI** — never Set Up Agent or Agents and keys
