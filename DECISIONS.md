# Decisions

The owner's rulings for this project, and where their records stand.

## The keeping rule

- A ruling enters this file on the day the owner rules, under its ruling day, newest first.
- An entry stands only in the owner's own words. No entry is composed from a summary or a paraphrase. A ruling whose words do not stand yet has no entry; the gap is named where it was found.
- A decision prepared for the owner is kept as its own file in docs/decisions/, one file per decision, in the shape the fleet's Decision Records Policy sets. The policy's canonical text stands in the agents-policy repo at docs/decision-records-policy.md; no copy of it stands here.
- When the owner rules, the decision's record gains the outcome, written as "chosen, because\u2026", and a Consequences section stating at least one cost as plainly as the benefits.
